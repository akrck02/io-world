# io-world

A little 2D puzzle-platformer: Io walks and jumps through a factory.

- `IoWorldDemo/`: the game, made with Godot (its history holds the first version, made with Unity).
- `IoWorldGo/`: its first level made again with [Go Engine](https://github.com/akrck02/go-engine), to play on the Nintendo 3DS too (see its README).
